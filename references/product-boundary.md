# Product Boundary

BFLabs Agent Readiness is an evidence-backed diagnosis and delivery system.

## Open-source surface

The free surface is the complete method for one website, not a preview of a paid product:

- facts, goals, and a frozen question set;
- a real baseline of how AI answers before any change meant to affect those answers;
- finding issues and the pages that own them;
- owner-gated local change and release;
- public retest of the same questions;
- AI-answer comparison;
- the method and templates for reviewing owner-supplied business data;
- a stage report and the next-round step;
- checks, templates, and the report format.

It also includes:

- local public-site diagnosis;
- traceable question and opportunity discovery without invented search volume;
- evidence-linked content specifications and Markdown with freshness gates;
- site-foundation planning when the owner has no site, asks only for a site, or needs missing foundation pages;
- offline aggregation of customer-supplied answer observations with explicit denominators;
- evidence-bounded technical SEO plans with official-source freshness and rollback gates;
- repository-local GEO audit and repair;
- WebMCP implementation guidance and verification contracts;
- deterministic examples, templates, checks, and receipts.

The complete method is not unlocked halfway. Learning materials, the free Skill, and a later paid engagement use the same method. Nobody has to finish a course, download every file, or buy a host before they can start.

The user brings their own Agent, site access, platform accounts or answer records, and business data. WorkBuddy's custom edition is a convenience host. It is never a prerequisite for the free method.

Do not add a paid sampling service, task engine, CMS, multi-tenant control plane, or auto-renewal to this surface. Do not move paid execution into the free Skill because a gap exists.

First real case: `https://global.beefapi.com/`, measured with English developer questions. `https://bufflow.app/` is the Chinese demo example only.

## Productized service surface

BFLabs performs the work:

- implementation across repositories and infrastructure;
- repeated real-platform sampling;
- hosted storage of the project record;
- monitoring, alerting, and evidence retention;
- ongoing review;
- customer-authorized conversion and revenue attribution when BFLabs is hired to do that work.

A missing capability in the free method is not permission to pull paid execution into the open Skill.

## 使用者能看见的责任区别

免费方法和付费工作用同一套做法。中途没有付费墙。没有网站时，也可以只要网站基础方案。

**付费首轮**由团队承担：整理事实、按约定改页面或采集、发布、真实复测，以及写出这一轮做了什么、依据是什么、还缺什么。通常交付事实包、问题与页面清单、改动和发布证据、复测、已有的业务依据，以及下一轮建议。价格、工期和具体改哪些页按当时范围另定。

**付费持续服务**覆盖约定期内的数据接入、持续采样、维护、复盘和下一轮执行。服务期、频次、访问权限和可交付内容以合同为准，不默认无限代维。

**网站基础按需纳入**。已有网站先补缺口，不重建。还没有网站时，给最小可实施网站和部署交接。实际搭建由使用者的 Agent 或既有建站工具完成。

付费不保证平台会推荐，也不保证收入会增长。完成一轮工作是可交付结果；没有客人从看到走到付钱，不能说获客已经成功。

Method vocabulary: `references/whitehat-method.md`. Site foundation: `references/site-foundation.md`.

## Claims

Allowed claims describe observed readiness, verified task calls, and measured evidence. Do not claim that a site has been indexed, cited, recommended, or converted unless the corresponding external evidence is recorded. Do not promise ranking, recommendation, traffic, or revenue growth.
