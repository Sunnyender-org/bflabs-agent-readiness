# BFLabs GEO Readiness design direction

## Visual thesis

Forensic, calm, exact. The interface should feel like a paper evidence ledger placed on a technical workbench: warm neutral canvas, ink-first typography, restrained oxide and moss signals, and no decorative AI gradients.

## Content and utility plan

- Intake states the public-scan boundary before submission.
- A single evidence rail presents Discoverable, Understandable, and Actionable as related but independent axes.
- Findings lead with failed predicates and evidence, not marketing advice or a dominant total score.
- AI visibility and business outcome remain visibly unmeasured.

## Interaction thesis

- Submission changes from a real staged progress list into the report, without fake percentages.
- Axis controls reveal evidence inline and preserve browser back/scroll behavior.
- Motion uses short opacity/translate transitions only and disappears under reduced-motion preferences.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `oklch(96% 0.012 72)`
- Paper: `oklch(99% 0.006 72)`
- Ink: `oklch(24% 0.018 48)`
- Muted ink: `oklch(49% 0.016 56)`
- Oxide: `oklch(49% 0.13 39)`
- Moss: `oklch(47% 0.075 145)`
- Radius scale: `4px`, `8px`, `14px`, `999px`
- Display: Iowan Old Style / Songti SC
- Body: Avenir Next / PingFang SC
