# BFLabs Agent Readiness design direction

## Canonical visual source

The committed Grok prototype under `designs/bflabs-ui-agent-readiness` is the visual source of truth. The production-candidate local scanner keeps its calm BFLabs landing, compact report, and staged delivery example while binding them to real scan results.

## Visual thesis

Quiet, precise, product-first. The first screen contains the BFLabs lockup, one question, one public URL field, and one action. Warm white, charcoal, and signal orange come from `@bflabs/ui`; no editorial folio, serif manifesto, decorative grid, gradient, or dashboard chrome is introduced.

## Content and utility plan

- Landing explains the bounded free check in one line.
- Report keeps Discoverable, Understandable, and Actionable as independent axes.
- Failed predicates stay above growth suggestions.
- Detailed evidence, Artifact Pack actions, child Skill routing, and the BFLabs service path stay behind secondary disclosure.
- AI visibility and business outcome remain visibly unmeasured.
- Delivery is explicitly an example and uses the baseline, repair, verify, trend lifecycle.

## Interaction thesis

- A real staged scan replaces the landing only after the report is ready.
- Report and delivery example use a compact two-tab workspace.
- “如何改进” reveals open-source and paid paths without crowding the initial report.
- Evidence remains available through an explicit disclosure.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable smooth transitions.

## Verification

- Desktop must match the density and hierarchy of `preview/01-diagnose.png`, `preview/02-report.png`, and `preview/03-delivery.png`.
- Verify the real scanner flow at desktop and 390px mobile width.
- Check no horizontal overflow, focus visibility, Chinese text fit, report tabs, disclosure controls, rescan, and console errors.
